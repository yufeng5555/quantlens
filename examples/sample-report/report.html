7 · Production limitations

  • The final holdout uses a one-time in-process capability and cross-run reveal ledger. It is not yet isolated under an independent OS principal or object-store ACL.
  • The control-plane anchor is write-once in the local database but is not yet protected by a production MAC or immutable external store.
  • Returns are split-adjusted price returns. Dividends and distributions are excluded; this can materially change both strategy and benchmark outcomes.
  • Uninvested cash earns 0% and Sharpe uses rf = 0. Long cash periods therefore do not include the return an investor might earn on cash or Treasury bills.
  • Commission and slippage are constant bps assumptions. Backtests exclude dynamic bid–ask spreads, liquidity, capacity, market impact, borrowing, taxes, and future regime changes.
  • The battery status does not apply an investment objective to final-holdout returns and must not be interpreted as strategy approval.
